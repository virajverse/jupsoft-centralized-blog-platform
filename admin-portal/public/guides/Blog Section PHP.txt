=====================================================
Jupsoft CMS Integration Guide for PHP / Laravel
=====================================================

1. Environment (.env):
JUPSOFT_API_URL="https://blogary.jupsoft.com/v1"
JUPSOFT_WEBSITE_ID="YOUR_WEBSITE_ID"
JUPSOFT_API_KEY="YOUR_API_KEY"

2. Client Helper (JupsoftCms.php):
$cms = new JupsoftCms();

// Listing:
$response = $cms->getBlogs($page, 9);
$blogs = $response['data'];

// Detail:
$response = $cms->getBlogBySlug($slug);
$article = $response['data'];

3. Output Article HTML:
echo '<h1>' . htmlspecialchars($article['title']) . '</h1>';
echo '<div class="content">' . $article['content'] . '</div>';
